Welcome to our school

Education that nurtures the whole child

Rudolf Steiner School Mbagathi is a learning community in Kenya inspired by the Waldorf approach to education.

We value the development of each child through meaningful relationships, practical experiences, creativity, academic learning and connection with the natural world.

Our school brings together children, teachers, families and the wider community in a shared journey of learning.

The school’s published vision is to nurture individuals in a diverse cultural environment who positively transform society. Its mission describes quality Steiner/Waldorf education that develops creativity, responsibility and independence in a healthy, safe and sustainable environment.

Our educational approach

Learning through every stage of childhood

The Waldorf approach recognises that children develop in different ways as they grow. Learning brings together thinking, feeling and practical activity.

EARLY CHILDHOOD

Play and discovery

Young children learn through imitation, imaginative play, movement, stories and exploring the world around them.

PRIMARY YEARS

Imagination and connection

Stories, art, music, movement and practical experiences help children connect with ideas and develop understanding.

ADOLESCENCE

Independent thinking

Older learners are encouraged to question, investigate, reason and develop their own perspectives.

How the school works

More about our learning community

The school's published information describes a connected approach to education, care, practical learning and community participation. Details below are drawn from the school's website and should be reconfirmed before official publication.

Learning stages

The official website describes four Kindergarten groups, Primary classes 1–8 and Junior Secondary classes 9–10. It presents the school as combining Steiner/Waldorf education with Kenya's Competency Based Curriculum (CBC).

Learning through experience

Published subjects and activities include mathematics, English, Kiswahili, French, science, geography and history, alongside music, poetry, storytelling, painting, handwork, woodworking, baking, swimming, sports and gardening.

Main Lesson

The school describes a morning Main Lesson of around two hours, with some subjects explored in focused blocks of approximately four weeks. Please confirm the current timetable with the school.

Boarding and care

The boarding page describes a family-like environment, two boarding houses and boarding parents who support children's daily routines. It refers mainly to Primary pupils and a small number of Kindergarten children. Availability and arrangements must be confirmed directly.

Farm and nature

The school's farm information describes vegetable growing, an orchard, cows, organic and biodynamic practices, and syntropic farming. Gardening and practical farm activities connect children with food, nature and sustainability.

Governance and community

The published governance information describes a Board of Trustees, a College of Teachers and school management roles. Parents are also invited to participate through class representatives, meetings and a parents' committee.

Teacher development

The school describes an in-house Waldorf/Steiner teacher-training programme for Kindergarten and Primary educators, including modules, mentorship and diploma pathways. Ask the school for current dates and eligibility.

School traditions

Published parent information refers to community celebrations including St John's Festival, Michaelmas and Advent, alongside class presentations, camps, trips and other school gatherings.

Parent partnership

The school's parent information discusses communication with teachers, parent consultations, class representatives, school routines, trips, homework, health and attendance. Families should request the latest handbook and policies.

This summary is based on information published by the school. Programmes, class structure, boarding, staffing, policies and training arrangements can change; please confirm current details with the school office.

Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

A few starting points for families considering the school. For current policies and availability, please check directly with the school.

What educational approach does the school follow?

The school is inspired by the Waldorf approach, which brings together academic learning, creativity, practical activity and attention to child development.

What learning stages does the school offer?

The official website describes four Kindergarten groups, Primary classes 1–8 and Junior Secondary classes 9–10. Contact the school to confirm current classes, entry points and availability.

What is the Main Lesson?

The school describes a morning Main Lesson of around two hours, with some subjects taught in focused blocks. Please ask the school for the current timetable.

Does the school have a farm?

The school describes a farm with vegetable growing, an orchard and livestock, alongside organic, biodynamic and syntropic farming practices. Ask the school about current activities and visits.

How can parents participate?

The school's parent information describes class representatives, parent meetings and a parents' committee, as well as communication with teachers. Request the current parent handbook for details.

Can families ask about boarding?

Yes. Families can contact the school to ask about boarding availability, arrangements, age requirements and current costs.

Does the school offer sponsorship?

The school has described sponsorship as part of its community support. Eligibility, availability and application arrangements must be confirmed with the school office.

How do I find out about fees?

Fees can change. Please request the current fee schedule directly from the school rather than relying on older information found online.

How can I arrange a visit or ask about admission?

Use the admissions enquiry form on this page or contact the school office by phone or email to ask about visits and the current application process.

Where is the school located?

The school is in the Mbagathi area of Nairobi. Use the directions link in the contact section and confirm arrangements with the school before travelling.
