Welcome to Mbagathi

Education for the whole human being.

At Rudolf Steiner School Mbagathi, education is about more than academic achievement. It is about nurturing the intellectual, emotional, creative and practical capacities of every child.

Inspired by the Steiner/Waldorf educational philosophy, our learning community seeks to help children develop curiosity, confidence, independence and a sense of responsibility towards others and the world.

Our Philosophy
Growing together, learning for life.
The Steiner Approach

Learning with head, heart and hands.

We value an education that brings together thinking, feeling and doing, giving children opportunities to discover their abilities and engage meaningfully with life.

✦

Learning from the Land

Rooted in nature. Growing with purpose.

The school farm provides a special opportunity for practical learning. Through gardening and engagement with the land, children can explore where food comes from and how people care for the environment.

Farming, sustainability and respect for nature form an important part of the school's wider educational experience.
